Create a new revised Chitkara University floating-island illustration in portrait 4:5, using the attached references.
INPUT ROLES: Images 1, 2, 3 are ART STYLE REFERENCES ONLY. Image 4 is the CAMPUS LAYOUT and architecture reference: use the left panel A and the bottom building photos. Do not copy any reference text.
STYLE CHANGE IS CRITICAL: emulate the graphic, illustrated isometric diorama language of the three style references: image 1's simple colourful geometric planes and toy people, image 2's neatly layered thick square tile foundation and readable isometric shapes (but do NOT use pixel art), image 3's delicate dark hand-inked outlines, cube trees, charming graphic detail and hard-edged cel shading. This must look like a drawn 2.5D editorial illustration, with large clear shapes and restrained detail, not a rendered 3D scene. No realistic lighting, texture, reflective roads, atmospheric clouds, photographic detail, or dense tiny windows. Keep ample plain indigo-violet negative space around the island. Night is expressed through the indigo-violet background, purple shadows, small flat golden windows and simple firefly halos, without cinematic lighting or extensive bloom.

How I used the tools

I developed the idea, photographed the campus and directed the design. Claude helped me write and refine the Three.js model. I used GPT to develop the interface assets and four concept images, and ElevenLabs for the voiceover. I published the demo on GitHub Pages. Uploads and Glows stay in each visitor’s browser; the people and meetings shown are examples.

I built the model from photographs and maps rather than a 3D scan. The reference images and early screen captures show how it developed.
